## 3. Candidate UI/render optimizations (top 5)

Orchestrator picks top-3 after measurement.

### 3.1 [HIGH] Group creature draws by color
**Location:** `web/src/render.ts:74-158 drawCreatures`. At 1500 creatures: ~9000 Canvas2D state-mutating calls per frame.

With M5 NN-hash color, pigment becomes quantized u32 — discrete and reuseable. Bucket creatures by `color int` into `Map<u32, number[]>`:
```ts
for (const [color, indices] of buckets) {
  ctx.fillStyle = colorToRGBString(color);
  ctx.beginPath();
  for (const i of indices) ctx.arc(sx[i], sy[i], r[i], 0, TAU);
  ctx.fill();
}
```
Expected save: **2-4 ms/frame at heavy load**. Rings (eye/move/scav/mouth/armor) go away with D9 — per-creature draw becomes 1 arc.

### 3.2 [HIGH] Off-screen creature cull
**Location:** `web/src/render.ts:95-158`. Insert 4-line viewport reject before `beginPath`. At tight zoom, only a fraction visible. Expected save: **1-3 ms/frame**.

### 3.3 [MED] `drawGrassMap` putImageData fast-path
**Location:** `web/src/render.ts:160-187`. With M2 (fixed alpha), hoist `fillStyle` outside loop. Bigger win: `putImageData` to off-screen canvas + drawImage. M1+M2 already deliver ~4× of this. Measure first.

### 3.4 [MED] Render-skip at high speeds
**Location:** `web/src/main.ts:285-332`. Every RAF (60Hz) renders regardless of speed. At speed=100, 200 ticks per RAF. Skip every other frame:
```ts
const renderEveryN = speed >= 100 ? 2 : 1;
if (frameCounter++ % renderEveryN === 0) { renderWorld(...); }
```
Expected save: **2-6 ms/frame budget freed at speed=100**.

### 3.5 [LOW-MED] Hoist DOM status + 1Hz polling out of RAF
Move `pollRail` to setInterval at 10-20 Hz, or rely on D4/D5 deletions reclaiming the cost.

### 3.6 [LOW] Cache `world.grass_buffer()` between frames at high speed
Captured by §3.4.

## 10. Acceptance check

Success if measurement shows **at least one of**:

A. **Render-time win at heavy load** — `frame > renderWorld` mean µs drops ≥ 30% at ~1500 creatures.
B. **TPS-stable improvement** — `world.tps` at speed=1 stays ≥ 90% of pre-M3 baseline.
C. **Subjective "feels faster" pass** — manual eyeballing shows smoother creature movement at speed=10/100.
D. **Jank count drops** — `jank_count` over 60s drops ≥ 50% vs baseline.

Acceptance: at least one of {A,B,C,D} measurably improves, AND none regress past baseline. If none improve, M3 reverts and defers to v1.4.
