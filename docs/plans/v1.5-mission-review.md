## Executive verdict

**Ship the mission. With material edits.**

The two structural wins — semantic 32-input brain + action-EMA colors — are correctly motivated, well-designed, and will deliver visible improvement. All three reviewers agree on this. But the mission as written has **three classes of problems** that the implementer will hit on contact:

1. **Magic numbers that don't survive arithmetic.** The grass-sector cost estimate is off by 4× on AABB cells (R2); the LUT size is off by 5× (R2); the He-init math is wrong for 2 of 3 layers (R1); the "graze overlaps ~8 cells" estimate is wrong (R3); the time estimate is off by 2-3× (R3).
2. **Silent omissions.** Render-path color surgery is underspecified; Inspector contents post-v1.5 unspecified; EMA initialization at birth unspecified; what happens to `last2_action`/`prev_energy`/`eye_trig`/`vision.rs` unspecified; ~20 broken tests not budgeted (R3). R8 conversion plumbing (Vec<u8> scratch) unspecified (R3).
3. **Two design choices that may sabotage the mission's own goals.** Curriculum with `factor=0` below `min_pop` *anaesthetizes selection* during the regime that most needs gradient (R1, R3 both flag). Removing color-as-NN-input while making color meaningful via EMA leaves prey unable to perceive predators (R1).

**One pleasant surprise** (R2): the renderer is already WebGL2 (commit `1f5692e` `feat(render): WebGL2 instanced renderer + sim restart button`). The mission's R8 + dirty-rect plan was framed as if it required moving away from Canvas2D — it doesn't. Grass texture is currently R32F uploaded via `texSubImage2D`; switching to R8 is a ~10-line edit, not a foundational migration. **Mission should be reframed accordingly.**

### Bottom line

- **Brain inputs + 2-layer + Leaky ReLU + tanh velocity heads:** ship as designed (with He-init fix).
- **Action-EMA colors:** ship as designed (spec birth initialization).
- **Grass cell-size 5u → 1.25u + R8 texture:** ship; defer dirty-rect to v1.5.1.
- **`N_CHUNKS` bump:** ship dynamically, not constant 16.
- **Worker utilization widget:** defer or rename ("chunks: A/B" not "workers active/total").
- **Curriculum subsystem:** *DO NOT SHIP AS DESIGNED*. Either rework with `MIN_FACTOR=0.25` floor, or defer to v1.6.
- **3 new sliders (max_age, split_threshold, split_gift):** ship; budget 1.5 hr not 30 min.
- **Right-edge settings overlay:** ship, but spec the inspector-collision interaction first.

Realistic time budget: **2 working days** (12-16 hours focused work). If you genuinely have half a day, cut to: brain + colors + grass-cell + 3 cost sliders. Drop curriculum, worker widget, settings-overlay relocation, dirty-rect.

## Critical issues (must address before implementation)

### C1. Curriculum sabotages its own goal at low pop

**Source:** R1 §"Concerns — high #1", R3 §"Concerns — high / Open questions #2".

**The problem:** The mission says factor goes to **0** below `min_pop=1000`. R1's analysis: this *deletes* the fitness gradient that the brain redesign exists to reward. With free upkeep, free movement, free eating — every random brain reaches `max_age` and splits some number of times before dying. **Brain weights become functionally invisible to evolution during the bootstrap regime.**

Additionally R1 predicts oscillation rather than equilibrium: pop crosses 1000 → costs ramp → marginal lineages die → pop drops below 1000 → costs vanish → everyone breeds → repeat.

**Recommendation (both reviewers agree):**
- Replace "factor goes to zero" with **"factor has a floor."** `factor = MIN_FACTOR + (1.0 - MIN_FACTOR) * smoothstep(min_pop, max_pop, current_pop)` with `MIN_FACTOR = 0.25`.
- Or defer curriculum to v1.6.
- Or consider **time-based ramp** (R1's open question #6): costs ramp 0→1 over first 5000 ticks then stay at 1. Avoids oscillation entirely.

### C2. Time budget is off by 2-3×

**Source:** R3 §"Reality check / Implementation order".

R3 sum of realistic per-step ranges: **~12.75 hours**, not "half a day." Largest underestimates:
- Step 1 "30 min quick wins" → realistic ~1 hr.
- Step 3 "color overhaul ~1 hr" → realistic ~2 hr (render-path surgery has 5+ touch sites).
- Step 5 "NN landing 2-3 hr" → realistic 4-6 hr (deletes vision.rs, rewrites build_nn_input, 18 layout constants, breaks ~10 layout tests + ~5-8 brain tests).

**Recommendation:** Re-budget to **2 working days** (12-16 hr). If half a day is the actual constraint, cut to brain + colors + grass-cell + 3 sliders — drop curriculum/worker-widget/settings-overlay/dirty-rect.

### C3. Magic numbers in the perf budget are wrong

**Source:** R2 §"High concerns / Summary numbers table".

| Mission claim | R2's verified value | Δ |
|---|---|---|
| AABB cell count at 40×40 unit / 1.25u | "~256" | **1024** (804 after circle-clip) | 4× worse |
| LUT size for 33×33 entries | "~1 KB" | **5.4 KB** (with f32 weight + u8 sector) | 5× larger |
| Grass-sector cost @ 1000 creatures, naive | "~13 ms" | **~50 ms** | 4× worse |
| Grass-sector cost @ 1000 creatures, optimized | "~80 μs" | regime-dependent: 10 μs sparse → **8 ms mid-game** | regime-dependent |

The optimization stack still gets to a workable budget, but the margin shrinks from "25×" to "6×" — and **mid-game grass coverage may bust the 2 ms target**. R2's mitigation: add a per-row "any cell non-empty" bitset (~480 bytes) so the scan can skip whole rows in O(1).

**Recommendation:** Rewrite the perf-budget paragraph with corrected numbers. Add an explicit dependency on the empty-skip rate so the budget can be verified empirically.

### C4. He-init math is wrong for 2 of 3 layers

**Source:** R1 §"Concerns — high #2".

Mission says: "Bump `NN_INIT_RANGE 0.3 → ~0.4` for the He-init regime."

He-init: `r = sqrt(6/fan_in)` for uniform `U(-r, +r)`.
- Layer 1 (fan_in=32): `r = 0.433` ✓ close to 0.4
- Layer 2 (fan_in=48): `r = 0.354`
- Layer 3 (fan_in=24): `r = 0.500`

Global `0.4` is too small for layer 3 (output logits underscaled → first-index-tiebreak biases toward fallback Graze at founder time).

**Recommendation:** Per-layer init ranges OR pick `0.5` as a defensible single value (R1's argument: under-scaled output logits at founder time bias action selection toward fallback Graze; better to overshoot layer 2 than undershoot layer 3).

### C5. Renderer is already WebGL2 — mission framing is stale

**Source:** R2 §"The R8 + Canvas2D question".

Mission frames R8 + `texSubImage2D` as if it requires moving from Canvas2D. **The renderer is already WebGL2** as of commit `1f5692e`. `web/src/render.ts` is now ~40 lines of camera math; the actual draw path is `web/src/render-gl.ts`. Grass texture is currently uploaded via `texSubImage2D` every frame at R32F.

**Recommendation:** Reframe the mission's grass-render section: "narrow the existing per-frame R32F upload to R8 + dirty-rect" instead of "introduce WebGL." The work is ~10 lines + a new wasm getter, not a foundational change.

### C6. Render-path color surgery is underspecified

**Source:** R3 §"Concerns — high / Render-path color surgery".

Mission says "SoA tracks EMA channels; replaces `color_rgb` hot-mirror in render pack." 7 touch sites that need explicit handling:

1. Add `color_r/g/b: Vec<f32>` (or `color_rgb: Vec<[f32; 3]>`) to `CreatureSoA`
2. Delete `CreatureSoA::color_rgb: Vec<u32>`
3. Delete `Brain::color_rgb` and `Brain::weight_hash` fields
4. **Initialize EMA at birth** — to what? (0,0,0)? (0.5,0.5,0.5)? inherited? Decision left vacant.
5. EMA update site — probably `bookkeeping_tail` (no obvious existing per-creature post-action loop)
6. Update both `creatures_buffer` and `pack_render_buffer` unpack
7. Inspector schema (mission silent on contents)

**Recommendation:** Add an explicit checklist for step 3. Decision-surface for EMA initialization needs an answer.

### C7. Inspector contents post-v1.5 — mission silent

**Source:** R1 §"Things missing", R3 §"Concerns — medium".

After v1.5: `color_rgb` and `weight_hash` are gone; `vision_range`, `eye_count`, `eye_trig` likely gone; trait-constant rows (photo_eff, eat_eff, armor, bite_reach) are noise post-D3.

**Recommendation (R3):** Spec new Inspector contents:
- Action EMA RGB triple
- Last action history (mini-ring buffer, TS-side OK)
- Wall proximity 4-tuple
- Cooldown ticks
- Drop trait-constant rows

## Important issues (should address before shipping)

### I1. Removing color-as-vision while making color meaningful — prey can't see predators

**Source:** R1 §"Concerns — high #6".

Once color-EMA reflects strategy (Red = Eat-success), color is genuinely informative — but the brain *cannot see it*. Prey has no signal "the close thing is a known eater." Sector-creature-proximity is "something is close," not "predator."

**Recommendation:** Add `nearest_creature_dominant_color` (1 slot, into padding) so prey can perceive predator intent. Cheap (~5 LOC), otherwise color-EMA is pure UX.

### I2. Multi-founder seeding (v1.4 E1) belongs in this mission

**Source:** R1 §"Things missing", R3 §"Open question #10".

Single-founder + 80% extinction = the *evaluability* of the brain redesign is at risk. v1.4-directions identified E1 as XS-effort, L-impact. Mission doesn't mention it.

**Recommendation:** Add `founder_count` slider (default 8). XS effort. Without it, you can't tell whether the new brain "works" or whether you just got an unlucky seed.

### I3. Action-EMA "Graze fallback" contamination

**Source:** R1 §"Concerns — medium #9".

Graze is the **fallback action** in `decode_action` when Eat is gated by cooldown. A successful predator spends ~50% of life in cooldown → reads as "Green + Red mixed," not pure Red.

**Recommendation:** Score color by **argmax output before validity fallthrough**, not the final action. ~5 LOC.

### I4. 8-sector aliasing without bilinear weighting causes flicker

**Source:** R1 §"Concerns — high #4".

Going from 24-sector v1.3 vision (15°/sector) to 8-sector (45°/sector) is a 3× resolution drop. Creatures at sector boundaries flicker between adjacent sectors as angle drifts.

**Recommendation:** Bilinear sector weighting — a creature at 50° contributes 0.78 to sector 1 (45°) and 0.22 to sector 2 (90°). Same data size, modest extra cost. Eliminates flicker.

Also: document that sectors are **world-aligned** (creatures have no heading state, only vx/vy).

### I5. NN landing should split into 2 commits

**Source:** R3 §"Recommended changes #5".

Mission says step 5 is atomic. R3 recommends splitting:
- Commit A: 2-hidden-layer Brain + Leaky ReLU + new init range, with existing 112 inputs (weight count stays 2,808 — only topology changes).
- Commit B: Input rewrite to 32 (deletes vision raycast, rewrites build_nn_input).

Each is testable and revertable. Drops the "all break together" risk.

### I6. Mid-game grass coverage may exceed 2 ms budget

**Source:** R2 §"Concerns — medium / C4".

Skip-empty optimization works great at sparse (early game) and saturated (50%+ coverage); fails in middle band (~20% coverage = 8 ms/tick).

**Recommendation:** Per-row "any cell non-empty" bitset (~480 bytes) on `GrassGrid`, regenerated cheaply in `step()`. Scan skips whole rows in O(1). Closes the mid-game gap.

### I7. `N_CHUNKS` should be dynamic on pop, not constant 16

**Source:** R2 §"Concerns — medium / C5".

At pop=100 with N_CHUNKS=16, each chunk = 7 creatures < 16 (cache line). False sharing on output columns + 23% rayon task-spawn overhead.

**Recommendation:** `chunks = clamp(pop / 32, 4, min(16, worker_count))`. Keeps cache-line ownership clean at all pop levels.

Also verify (R2 open question #1): what does `wasm-bindgen-rayon` initThreadPool currently use? If hardcoded 8, bumping N_CHUNKS does nothing until JS init changes too.

### I8. Drop dead `flag_eye/move/mouth/armor` slots from `creatures_buffer`

**Source:** R3 §"Things missing #11".

All 4 flag slots are constant `1,1,1,0` post-D3 — already vestigial. Mission's renderer doesn't read them. `creature_stride` would drop from 10 to 6 (or 7 with new EMA color encoding). Opportunistic cleanup.

### I9. Capture today's tick breakdown before landing #6

**Source:** R2 §"High concerns / H4".

The "≤2 ms grass-sector at 1000 creatures" target floats in vacuum without a baseline. A 2-hour spike on `profile_report_json()` at pop=1000 resolves whether the budget is real.

## Lesser issues (worth knowing, not blocking)

- **L-ReLU justification.** R1 §C3: "dying neurons" is a *training* pathology; the right argument is "preserves sign information under random perturbation." Ship L-ReLU but rewrite the justification.
- **`ticks_since_last_split` clamp.** R1 §C8: divisor of 100 saturates well before next viable split (creature recovers in ~294 ticks). Either tie to `SPLIT_THRESHOLD/UPKEEP_BASE` or document as 100-tick refractory.
- **Color encoding has black-on-dark issue.** R1 §C10: floor each channel at 0.15 for display.
- **R8 quantization may band visually with `gl.LINEAR`.** R2 §L1: no action for v1.5 since renderer uses `gl.NEAREST`.
- **Memory growth +1.96 MB.** R2 §L3: 230k cells × 4 bytes × 2 (density + scratch) + R8 mirror. Comfortable for wasm32 4 GB limit.
- **`grass_buffer()` shape change** under R8 — Vec<u8> scratch needed. R2 §L2, R3 §"Things missing #16".
- **TPS dropdown styling needs `option { background; color }` CSS** (R3 §"Concerns — medium / TPS"). Mission's diagnosis is right; fix needs the `option {}` rule on Linux/Firefox where OS-rendered options can render white-on-white.
- **Right-edge overlay collides with inspector** (R3 §"Concerns — medium / Right-edge"). Pick one: overlay-over, push-left, or open-from-left. 10-min decision but jank if left unmade.
- **Profiler spans** for new paths: `tick.proximity.walls`, `tick.proximity.creatures`, `tick.proximity.grass`, `tick.color_ema`. R3 §"Things missing #8".
- **`POPULATION_MILESTONES`, `first_move_fired`, `first_eat_fired`** are vestigial since D4. R3 §"Things missing #13-14": opportunistic cleanup if touching World struct.
- **Dirty-rect tracking, single bounding rect, is a trap** for scattered consumption. R2 §C2: use per-row dirty bits, or just whole-frame R8 (still within budget).
- **`creatures.last2_action` and `prev_energy` SoA columns** become dead. R1, R3 both flag — delete or document as reserved.
