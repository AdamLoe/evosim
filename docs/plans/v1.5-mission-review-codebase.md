# v1.5 mission review — codebase fit (R3)

## TL;DR

The mission is largely well-grounded in the actual codebase, but the **"half a day"** estimate is fantasy — this is realistically 2-4 focused days of work, with the grass scale-up and NN rewrite each capable of eating a full day alone. The mission has three concrete fact-bugs (claimed grass cell-size is 2.5u not 5u in one comment; cites `~230k cells` which is correct only if `WORLD_SIZE` is unchanged at 600 and cell-size is 1.25u; misstates grass cell count in `GRASS_GRID_DIM` comment); and silently omits several things that will trip the implementer (Inspector content post-removal of weight-hash color, render-path color hot-mirror surgery, brain-pack format change in `creatures_buffer`/`pack_render_buffer`, profiler spans for new paths, JS-side settings schema migration). The two structural wins — semantic NN inputs and action-EMA colors — are correctly motivated and will deliver. Bump the budget, sequence the NN landing more carefully, and explicitly call out the render-path color surgery before shipping this mission.

## What the mission gets right

- **Locked-in NN input semantics are well-thought-out.** Positive-signal encoding (hunger ↑ instead of energy ↓) is genuinely easier for tanh/ReLU heads to learn. The 8-sector creature/grass proximity blocks are a strong upgrade over 24-ray RGB raycasting that the network had to manually deconvolve.
- **The action-EMA color scheme is exactly the right replacement** for NN-hash colors. Drops the strobe-on-mutation problem and turns the visual into a strategy fingerprint. The asymmetric rates (+0.5 on Split, +0.1 per bite, +0.01 on Graze) make the three signals visible at different timescales — well calibrated.
- **Weight-count parity (2,808 either way) is genuinely a happy coincidence** and worth noting — it lets the implementer keep `NN_WEIGHT_COUNT` semantics stable and not redo every test that asserts it.
- **Grass-sector perf plan is sound.** The LUT for `(cell_dx, cell_dy) → (sector_id, 1/dist²)` is the obvious win and the mission correctly identifies that atan2 in the hot loop would explode. The "skip empty cells" early-out is the other 95% win.
- **Curriculum design correctly avoids duplicating sliders** by reusing the existing cost multipliers as the *target*, with `factor = smoothstep(min_pop, max_pop, pop)` as the gate. This is a strictly cleaner architecture than the "target value + active value" pattern.
- **Out-of-scope list is appropriately ruthless** — explicitly rejecting Rust server backend, WebGPU, NEAT, ecology tuning. Keeps the mission shippable.
- **Implementation order broadly correct**: quick wins → infra → color → curriculum → NN → grass. NN before grass is right (the new grass-sector input scales with cell density, so doing #6 first would force a perf rework of #5 right after).
- **R8 + dirty-rect upload** is the correct grass-bandwidth plan for 230k cells. R32F at 230k = 920 KB/frame at 60 Hz = ~55 MB/s, which is hot; R8 dirty-rect should easily hit the ≤25 MB/s target.

## Concerns — high

### Render-path color surgery is underspecified

**What the mission says:** "SoA tracks EMA channels; replaces `color_rgb` hot-mirror in render pack." (~1 hr.)

**What's actually in the code:**
- `CreatureSoA::color_rgb: Vec<u32>` (`src/creature.rs:59`) — packed 0x00RRGGBB.
- `Brain::color_rgb` and `Brain::weight_hash` (`src/brain.rs`) — set in `Brain::founder`, recomputed in `Brain::child_from` (`brain.rs:198`).
- `CreatureSoA::push` mirrors `brain.color_rgb` (`creature.rs:130`).
- `wasm_api.rs::creatures_buffer` unpacks `color_rgb` into 3 floats at offsets 3,4,5 (`wasm_api.rs:194-198`).
- `wasm_api.rs::pack_render_buffer` does the same unpack in the GPU instance pack (`wasm_api.rs:267-269`).
- `wasm_api.rs::creature_inspect_json` emits `color_rgb` as `#RRGGBB` and `weight_hash` as 16-hex (`wasm_api.rs:556-557`).
- Inspector reads both (`web/src/rail/inspector.ts:62-63`, `web/index.html:44-47`).

**Concrete recommendation:** The mission should explicitly list:
1. Add `color_r, color_g, color_b: Vec<f32>` to `CreatureSoA` (3 columns), or `color_rgb: Vec<[f32; 3]>` (1 column).
2. Delete `color_rgb: Vec<u32>` column (or repurpose into the packed render buffer at write time).
3. Delete `Brain::color_rgb` and `Brain::weight_hash` fields (no longer used). Inspector rows must go too.
4. Initialize EMA channels at birth — to what? Mission doesn't say. Probably `(0, 0, 0)` (start dark) or maybe `(0.5, 0.5, 0.5)` (start neutral). User-facing call.
5. EMA update site: end-of-tick after `action_this_tick` is resolved. There isn't an obvious existing per-creature post-action loop other than `bookkeeping_tail` — could add it there.
6. Update both `creatures_buffer` and `pack_render_buffer` to read the new columns.
7. Inspector schema bump: remove `color_rgb`/`weight_hash` from `CreatureInspectJson`, swap to `(r, g, b)` triple or a packed action_strategy readout.

This is closer to 2 hours, not 1, and the "where do we initialize" question is design-surface the mission left out.

### NN landing is way bigger than "2-3 hrs"

**What the mission says:** Step 5 ~2-3 hrs to land new inputs + 2 hidden layers + Leaky ReLU + grass-sector LUT.

**What's actually in the code:**
- `src/brain.rs` has SIMD-optimized `forward()` with `INPUT_CHUNKS = NN_INPUTS / 8 = 14` and `HIDDEN_CHUNKS = NN_HIDDEN / 8 = 3` (`brain.rs:34-36`). New shape `32→48→24→5` has 32, 48, 24, 5 — `48/8 = 6` and `24/8 = 3` and `32/8 = 4` divide cleanly, **but `5` doesn't** (this is fine for the output layer since it's the last matmul; brain.rs already handles this). However the SIMD chunk constants need to be reshaped.
- `forward` and `forward_scalar` (SIMD-vs-scalar parity test) both need full rewrites for a 2-hidden-layer pyramid.
- Layout constants in `constants.rs:113-145` (10 distinct offsets) all get deleted and redefined.
- `build_nn_input` (`world/nn.rs:196-260`) is rewritten end-to-end.
- `vision.rs` becomes mostly dead — the 24-sector RGB raycast (`VisionPass::run`) gets deleted or repurposed for the 8-sector creature_proximity pass. The vision cache (`World::vision`) probably needs resizing or removal.
- Sector-LUT scratch buffer goes into `World` (`mod.rs` field).
- Wall-proximity × 4: pure arithmetic, easy.
- Creature-proximity × 8: needs grid scan + sector binning.
- Grass-sector × 8 with LUT: new module probably.
- Tests broken: `vision_layout_matches_nn_input_block` (`nn.rs:359`), `nn_input_layout_self_state_correct`, `nn_input_layout_vision_passthrough`, `nn_input_patch_*` (4 tests), `nn_input_patch_center_slot_is_92`, plus the `decode_action_*` tests assume 3-logit slice (unchanged — good). Roughly **10 tests** to update + 5-10 net-new tests for new inputs.
- `creatures.last2_action` and `prev_energy` SoA columns become dead (mission's new self-state block has `prev_vx/vy` and EMA-like signals but no `prev_energy` delta or `last2_action`). Either keep them dead, or delete them — but deletion ripples through `creature.rs::push/remove_indices/clone_for_test`.

**Concrete recommendation:** Re-scope step 5 to 4-6 hours, and either explicitly defer deletion of `last2_action`/`prev_energy` to a follow-up or include that work in the estimate. Also: pre-write a checklist of every test that fails and its disposition — saves an hour of "wait, why is this red."

### Test suite impact is larger than implied

There are **119 `#[test]` cases** across `src/`. The mission says "All existing Rust tests still pass (or are updated)" without quantifying. From a scan:

**Will need rewriting (test counts, approximate):**
- NN input layout tests in `src/world/nn.rs`: ~9 tests reference `NN_INPUTS=112`, `NN_VISION_LEN=72`, `NN_GRASS_PATCH_OFFSET=80`, `NN_GRASS_PATCH_CENTER_SLOT=92`. All need new constants.
- `src/brain.rs` weight-layout, forward-pass, SIMD-vs-scalar parity, mutation tests: ~5-8 tests reference `NN_WEIGHT_COUNT==2808` (still true coincidentally) and layout `[w_ih | w_ho]` — new layout is `[w_ih | w_h1h2 | w_h2o]`. All weight-layout tests need rewrites.
- `src/vision.rs`: 5+ tests assume `VISION_LEN=72` and RGB raycasting semantics. Mostly dead post-mission.
- `src/creature.rs`: `eye_trig_has_24_active_sectors` and friends remain valid (eye_trig is for vision sector geometry — if vision pass is gutted, `recompute_eye_trig_at` may itself be deleted).
- `src/world/mod.rs`: `eye_trig_recomputed_on_birth` (line 590) depends on eye_trig being computed; if vision is rewritten, may need rework.

**Will probably stay green:**
- Grass tests (`src/grass.rs`, 15+ tests) — cell-size change just bumps numbers, but several tests hardcode `GRASS_CELL_SIZE` math (e.g. `let cx = (ix + 0.5) * GRASS_CELL_SIZE`) — those auto-scale. ✅
- Grid tests (`src/grid.rs`) — unchanged.
- Profiler, RNG — unchanged.

**Rough estimate: 20-30 tests broken, ~half need actual rewrites (not just constant updates).** Mission should call this out so the implementer doesn't try to ship with `cargo test` red.

## Concerns — medium

### `max_age` / `split_threshold` / `split_gift` sliders — call-site count

**What the mission says:** Add three sliders. (Implied: trivial — bundled in step 1's "~30 min".)

**What's actually in the code:**
- `SPLIT_THRESHOLD` (`constants.rs:23`): used at 2 production sites (`world/mod.rs:317,320`), 2 test sites (`world/tick.rs:410,500`), 1 dispatch site (`world/nn.rs:268`). All read directly from the constant.
- `SPLIT_GIFT_MAX` (`constants.rs:22`): 1 production site (`world/mod.rs:321`).
- `FOUNDER_MAX_AGE` (`constants.rs:63`): 3 production sites (`world/tick.rs:282,283`, `world/nn.rs:213`, `wasm_api.rs:543`), 1 test reference (`world/nn.rs:384`).

**Implementation work:**
1. Add 3 fields to `DevSliders` with defaults (`world/mod.rs:19-49`).
2. Add 3 `apply_*` helpers + 3 typed setters in `wasm_api.rs` (~30 LOC).
3. Add 3 entries to `try_set_slider` dispatch table (`wasm_api.rs:431-448`).
4. Replace 4 production read sites with `self.sliders.X` (mind the `is_valid_action` signature in `nn.rs:264-269` — it takes raw `energy` and `cooldown` not the world; either pass through, or convert the const-read to a parameter).
5. Add 3 fields to `Settings` interface + DEFAULTS (`web/src/settings.ts`).
6. Add 3 slider rows + reapply lines (`web/src/widgets/devpanel.ts`).
7. Update Inspector JSON `max_age` field reader if `FOUNDER_MAX_AGE` was the live source.

`is_valid_action(act, energy, cooldown)` takes `energy` as a raw float, compares to `SPLIT_THRESHOLD`. To make it slider-driven, you either pass `split_threshold` as a 4th arg (touches all 3 call sites + 6 tests) or refactor to take `&DevSliders`. Either is fine but it's not free.

**Recommendation:** Realistically 30-45 minutes per slider × 3 = 1.5 hr, not "bundled in 30 min for all UX work."

### Right-edge overlay vs existing DOM layout

**What the mission says:** "Settings panel moved to right-edge overlay (~320px wide, scrollable, opens via ⚙)."

**What's in the code:**
- `#right-rail` (`web/index.html:55`, `styles.css:87-100`) is *already* a 280px right-edge fixed panel, but **`display: none`** at `styles.css:104` (intentionally hidden post-v1.1 simplification per the comment).
- `#inspector-box` is *already* top-right pinned (`styles.css:154-164`, max-width 280px).
- `#devpanel-box` is positioned bottom-right (`styles.css:332-337`).

**The collision risk:** If the new settings overlay is 320px wide on the right edge, it overlaps with the inspector (also right-pinned at top, 280px wide). The mission doesn't say what happens to either widget when both want the right edge.

**Concrete recommendation:** Spec the interaction:
- Either: settings overlay slides over inspector (z-index higher, inspector inaccessible while open).
- Or: settings overlay shifts inspector to its left edge.
- Or: settings opens from the left, inspector stays right.

This is a 10-minute design decision but if left unmade, the implementer ships something janky.

Density values are `f32 ∈ [0, 1]` today; need to scale to `u8 ∈ [0, 255]` at upload time. **The R8 conversion needs a `Vec<u8>` scratch buffer in `WorldHandle`** to convert before upload. Mission glosses over this — adds 15 min and a `grass_buffer_u8` wasm function.
17. **Dirty-rect tracking** — mission says "Rust tracks the bounding rect of changed cells per tick." This is a new field in `GrassGrid` updated by `step()` and `consume()`. ~30 min of plumbing not in the estimate.

## Things that should be cut from scope

- **Dirty-rect upload as part of v1.5.** The R8 switch alone gets you 4× bandwidth reduction (1 byte vs 4); the dirty-rect *adds* implementation complexity for further reduction. Ship R8 + full-frame `texSubImage2D` first; defer dirty-rect to v1.5.1 or later if bandwidth measurably hurts. Saves ~1 hr and a class of "did I miss a write site" bugs.
- **Worker-utilization widget.** It's a nice diagnostic but it's the lowest-leverage item in the mission. The user is supposed to bump `N_CHUNKS` from 8 to 16; whether the widget shows "16/16 active" or "12/16 active" is interesting once during tuning and then ignored. Defer.
- **Curriculum subsystem.** This is the most "is this actually what the user wants" item. The decision that "if pop collapses below min_pop, costs back off to zero so the population can recover" is intentional but produces a sim where extinction is essentially impossible — which directly contradicts the "evolve to higher peaks" goal (lower selection pressure = slower convergence). Worth a sanity-check conversation with the user before shipping. Could trivially be deferred to v1.6 once the brain rewrite is stable.
